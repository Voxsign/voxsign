# VoxSign

Local-first voice AI platform. Hold to talk on iOS and Android; an open
Apache-2.0 Harness runtime routes each conversation on-device or through the
cloud of your choice. Your voice stays with you by default — a green or red
status dot always shows where your words are being processed.

- **For end users:** a calm voice companion that respects where your words go.
- **For developers:** an open runtime you can host, fork, and wire to any model.
- **For enterprises:** a voice layer you can run on your own infrastructure.

## Brand

Mission, positioning, keywords, values, and visual identity live in
[BRAND.md](BRAND.md). Every piece of public copy — including voxsign.ai —
derives from that document.

Four ideas every VoxSign surface reinforces:

1. **Local-first** — The device is the default. The cloud is an opt-in choice.
2. **Voice as input** — Speak the way you think. No typing, no menus.
3. **Privacy by design** — Voice leaves the phone only when you flip the switch.
4. **Open source** — Apps, Harness, and server are Apache-2.0. Fork and run it.
