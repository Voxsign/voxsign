# VoxSign — Brand Philosophy

_Version 1.0 · Internal brand reference · All copy on voxsign.ai derives from this document._

---

## Mission

Make voice the fastest, most private way to talk to your own software — on your own device.

We believe a microphone should be a first-class input method, not a feature bolted on
after the fact. VoxSign exists so that people can think out loud and have their local
machines listen, understand, and respond — without shipping every spoken sentence to a
data center by default.

## Positioning

**VoxSign is a local-first voice AI platform.**

It is not a cloud assistant. It is not a keyboard replacement. It is the layer between
your voice and your machines: a hold-to-talk surface on iOS and Android, paired with an
open Harness runtime that routes each conversation either on-device or through the cloud
of your choice. Every user can see — with a single green or red status dot — where their
voice is being processed right now.

- **For end users:** a calm, reliable voice companion that respects where your words go.
- **For developers:** an open runtime (Apache-2.0) you can host, fork, and wire to any model.
- **For enterprises:** a voice layer you can run on your own infrastructure, with no
  upstream vendor owning your transcripts.

## Core Keywords

Four ideas that every piece of VoxSign copy should reinforce.

1. **Local-first** — The device is the default. The cloud is an opt-in, per-session choice.
2. **Voice as input** — Speak the way you think. No typing, no menus, no modal walls.
3. **Privacy by design** — Voice leaves the phone only when you flip the switch. The
   runtime is open source so the claim can be audited, not just trusted.
4. **Open source** — Apps, Harness, and server are Apache-2.0. Fork it, run it, ship it.

## Values

- **Honesty about where data goes.** A visible green/red status per session is not optional UI.
- **Fewer words, faster thinking.** Hold to talk, release to send. Auto-named sessions.
- **Boring engineering, delightful interaction.** The runtime should be invisible; the
  microphone button should feel alive.
- **Build in the open.** We ship the Harness because voice infrastructure should not be
  a moat.
- **Respect the user's machine.** Local models are a first-class citizen, not a fallback.

## Voice & Tone

- Direct, engineering-clean. No hype words ("revolutionary", "AI-powered magic").
- Short sentences. Active verbs.
- Speak to builders and to curious end users in the same voice.
- Never invent benchmarks, customers, or partnerships that do not exist.

## Visual Identity

- **Mark:** rounded square containing an audio waveform — voice, captured.
- **Color:** deep charcoal background (`#0b0f14`), cyan accent (`#22d3ee`),
  local green (`#10b981`), cloud red (`#ef4444`).
- **Type:** system sans-serif stack; hierarchy comes from size and weight, not decorative fonts.
- **Motif:** the waveform repeats — in the logo, in the hero, in the hold-to-talk affordance.
- **Dark by default.** The light mode is an adaptation, not the primary experience.

## What VoxSign is not

To keep the brand tight, the website deliberately does **not** claim:

- A keyboard replacement, "five input modes", or any "NorthStar" scoring concept.
- A vertical-industry pitch (e.g. logistics, GCC) — the product is general-purpose voice.
- Cloud-first intelligence that happens to have an offline mode.
- A walled-garden assistant that locks you into one model.
