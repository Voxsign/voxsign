What it does

Speak. Your machine listens.

A calm voice layer between you and your devices — built on the open Harness runtime, not a black box.

Hold to talk

Press the big waveform button and speak. Release to send. No menus, no typing — just voice, the way you already talk.

Sessions, roles, domains

Run parallel conversations, each with its own role and domain. Work, personal, experiments — never bleed into one another.

Local ↔ cloud switch

One tap moves a session between your machine and the cloud. Green means on-device, red means routed out — you always know.

Local Cloud

Auto-named sessions

Threads title themselves from your first turns. No more "New chat 47" — just open the one you mean.

VoxSign Harness — the open runtime

The brains ship as Apache-2.0 software: session state, model routing, local/cloud transport, and the hold-to-talk protocol. Point it at your own model, your own endpoint, or your own server.

Privacy by design

Your voice is yours.

  • Speech is processed on-device before anything leaves the phone.
  • You choose, per session, whether to route through the cloud.
  • The runtime is open source — read the code, don't trust a claim.
  • No account required to use local models.
